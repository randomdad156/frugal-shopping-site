Delete your Frugal Shopping account

Last updated: 19 October 2026

You can delete your Frugal Shopping account and all its data at any time, in the app or without it.

In the app

Open Account → Delete account, read what will be deleted, and confirm. It happens right away.

Without the app

Send an email from the address you use to sign in to adrian.vaiesu+frugalshopping@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my Frugal Shopping account”.

We’ll confirm by replying to that address and delete the account within 7 days at the latest (usually the same day). If you sign in with Apple using a hidden email, write from the email linked to your Apple ID and mention it.

What is deleted

  • Your account (email, name, linked Google and Apple sign-ins)
  • Your lists and their products, including lists you shared with others (they lose access too)
  • Price alerts, favorite stores, remembered categories, list order, push notification tokens, your plan

What is kept

Nothing linked to you. Our hosting provider may keep encrypted backups for a short time before they are overwritten. Lists other people shared with you stay with their owners. If you bought Premium through Google Play, Google keeps its own purchase records; cancel the subscription in Google Play, since deleting the account doesn’t cancel it.
