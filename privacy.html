Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Frugal Shopping is a shopping-list app that matches your list with current supermarket offers in Romania. It is made by Adrian Vaiesu, an individual developer in Romania, who is the data controller for the data described here. Contact: adrian.vaiesu+frugalshopping@gmail.com.

In short: we collect only what the app needs to work, we don’t sell your data, there are no ads and no analytics or tracking, and you can delete your account and all its data at any time.

Legal basis (GDPR)

We process your account and list data to provide the service you asked for (contract, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Location and notifications are used only with your permission (consent, Art. 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time in Android’s settings. The account and security log, the shared-list activity and the record of your agreement are based on our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)) in handling complaints and disputes, keeping accounts secure and showing what was agreed; you can object by writing to us.

Security

All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access to your lists is limited by database rules to you and the people you share them with. Signing in is handled by Google or Apple, including their two-step verification.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can access, correct, export or delete your data, restrict or object to its processing, and withdraw consent (for location and notifications) at any time. Most of this is in the app:

  • Get a copy of your data (access and portability, Art. 15 and 20 GDPR): Account → Download my data. You get a JSON file, a common machine-readable format, with your account, plan, the terms you accepted, lists, products, alerts, preferences, your account log and what you did on shared lists.
  • Delete your account and data (Art. 17): Account → Delete account, or as described on this page.
  • Correct your lists and preferences directly in the app; your name and email come from your Google or Apple account.

For anything else, write to adrian.vaiesu+frugalshopping@gmail.com. We answer within one month (extendable by two months for complex requests, in which case we tell you why). You can also complain to the Romanian data protection authority, ANSPDCP (dataprotection.ro).

Deleting your data, and what we keep for 6 months

Deleting your account immediately and permanently removes your lists and their products (including lists you shared with others), price alerts, preferences, favorite stores, sign-ins and notification devices. Lists other people shared with you aren’t deleted; you just leave them.

We keep a minimal record for 6 months: your account id, email address, sign-up and deletion dates, your plan and purchase history, which sign-in methods you used, the terms you accepted and your account log entries. Nothing from your lists. We keep it only to establish, exercise or defend legal claims, such as a payment dispute, a refund or chargeback, or abuse of Premium or account linking (Art. 17(3)(e) and our legitimate interest under Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Nobody uses it for anything else, and it’s deleted automatically 6 months after the account was deleted. Our hosting provider may keep encrypted backups for a short time before they are overwritten.

Deleting the account doesn’t cancel a store subscription: cancel it in Google Play or the App Store.

What can and can’t be recovered

We don’t keep copies of your lists, so most deletions are final:

  • A deleted product can be brought back with Undo for a few seconds right after you delete it. After that it’s gone.
  • Checked-off products stay in “Purchased” until you clear them; clearing them can also be undone for a few seconds.
  • A deleted list can’t be recovered, by you or by us. The account log only notes that it was deleted (its title and how many products it had), not what was on it. On a shared list, the Activity shows the names of products removed in the last 30 days, so they can be added again by hand.
  • A deleted account can’t be recovered. The minimal record kept for 6 months can’t be used to restore the account or its lists.
  • Our hosting provider’s backups exist only to recover from an outage; we don’t restore individual accounts or lists from them.

Want your own copy? Use Account → Download my data whenever you like.
